Welcome to SnowyStudio

Paint your own F-16 in 3D

Actual War Thunder model + matching UV atlas · Works locally in Chrome / Edge

  1. Left-click and drag on the F-16 to paint directly onto its surface. The strokes follow its UV mapping, even across nearby triangles.
  2. Right-drag to rotate the plane, scroll to zoom; use Top / Right / Left / Bottom to inspect difficult places.
  3. In Decals, choose a stamp or upload a transparent PNG. Hover over the 3D model to preview the exact placement; resize and rotate before left-clicking to apply. Esc cancels without painting.
  4. Select Alpine or Arctic for instant winter camouflage. Choose the mountain brush for hand-painted lines.
  5. Use Undo / Redo freely. Save a .snowyproject.json to continue painting later.
  6. Press Export War Thunder skin for a ZIP containing the 4K .tga and .blk. Extract the skin folder into War Thunder/UserSkins.

Paint applies to the main fuselage, wings and tail surface. Some cockpit and weapon components use separate materials; those are displayed as reference geometry, not painted into the main texture.
